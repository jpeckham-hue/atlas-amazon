# v0.11 plan (draft): one official demand-data source

> Draft for discussion. Nothing here is implemented, and v0.11 starts only after
> the v0.10 live catalog slice has been recorded and replayed
> ([sp-api-setup.md](sp-api-setup.md)).
>
> **Status (2026-10-04):** v0.10 is paused until SP-API credentials are
> provisioned. v0.11 is not started. The source choice below depends on
> which access the owner confirms.

## Decided

* **Marketplace:** US only for now.
* **Cadence:** weekly demand data is enough. Real-time and daily tracking
  are out of scope for this stage.

## Goal

v0.10 adds market evidence (what competitor listings say) but no demand
evidence. New catalog candidates such as "leak proof straw" stay unscored on
demand. v0.11 should add exactly one official, read-only source of demand
signals. It follows the v0.10 discipline: Evidence with raw payloads,
record/replay, a call plan with a hard cap, offline CI, and missing signals
kept explicitly missing.

## Scope (if Brand Analytics)

1. **Provider.** A `KeywordDataProvider` implementation that requests,
   polls and downloads one weekly US Search Terms report. It filters locally to the run's candidate keywords and emits
   `keyword_metric` Evidence holding `search_frequency_rank`, period, report
   ID and raw rows.
2. **Call plan.** Fixed calls per report: create, poll (capped) and
   download. The poll count is part of the cap. No per-call fee. The report
   file size is printed in the plan once known.
3. **Signal mapping.** Rank feeds a monotonic demand transform, documented in
   the score contributions. Atlas never converts rank to an invented
   volume. Terms absent from the report keep demand missing.
4. **Integration.** Demand applies only to keywords present in the report.
   Market opportunity, product relevance and product truth stay separate,
   and the support gate is unchanged.
5. **Live slice.** One report for the two scenarios' keywords, recorded to a
   fresh `live/demand_v11/` directory, with comparison against fixture
   metrics: confirmed, absent, rank-shifted.
6. **Tests.** Report parsing, polling caps and timeouts, partial or empty
   reports, replay exactness, marketplace scoping, the "no invented volume"
   invariant, and no network in CI.

## Not in v0.11

Multiple demand providers, Ads bid optimization, listing writes,
publishing, a CLI, or any change to semantic prompts and thresholds.
